\caption{Sequence diagram for the three-agent CRSC evaluation pipeline.
\textbf{Phase~I}: the adversary injects a backdoor trigger $\gamma$ at rate $\rho$; the
model is fine-tuned (SFT or RLHF), yielding $\theta_{\text{ft}}$.
\textbf{Phase~II}: the CRSC Engine sends a fixed probe set $\mathcal{T}$ ($K\!=\!500$) to
both checkpoints and collects last-layer hidden states and loss sequences.
\textbf{Phase~III}: $\Delta_{\text{hidden}}$ (Frobenius drift) and $\Delta H$ (entropy
shift) are computed internally and combined into the CRSC score.
\textbf{Phase~IV}: the score is broadcast to three specialized agents that perform
detection (Agent~1: $\text{F1}\!=\!0.891$), trend analysis (Agent~2:
$r\!=\!0.847$), and automated response (Agent~3: Precision@$\tau\!=\!0.903$),
whose outputs feed a structured risk report.}
\label{fig:sequence}
