\caption{CRSC evaluation protocol (sequence diagram, Variant~B --- six-participant full pipeline).
\textit{Phase~I} (Adversarial Setup): the threat actor injects a backdoor trigger~$\gamma$
and initiates SFT\,/\,RLHF fine-tuning ($\theta_{\text{base}}\!\to\!\theta_{\text{ft}}$).
\textit{Phase~II} (Probe Evaluation): the CRSC Engine dispatches the fixed probe
set~$\mathcal{T}$ ($K\!=\!500$, seed\,42) and receives hidden-state vectors
$h_{\text{base}},h_{\text{ft}}$ and loss sequences $\ell_{\text{base}},\ell_{\text{ft}}$.
\textit{Phase~III} (Metric Computation): the engine computes $\Delta_{\text{hidden}}$
and $\Delta H$, then broadcasts the CRSC threshold query to all three agents.
\textit{Phase~IV} (Agent Evaluation): Agent~1 (Detection, F1\,=\,0.891),
Agent~2 (Analysis, $r\!=\!0.847$), and Agent~3 (Response, Prec@$\tau\!=\!0.903$)
return their verdicts; the engine consolidates into a structured risk report.}
